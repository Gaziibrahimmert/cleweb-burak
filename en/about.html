About me

Burak Arkan

Psychologist (M. Sc.) · Sociotherapist · Psychological psychotherapist in training

Hello, my name is Burak Arkan.

I am a psychologist and sociotherapist with many years of professional experience. Since 2009 I have worked in various settings of psychological counselling, psychosocial support and mental health care.

In my work I accompany people in a wide range of life situations and with many kinds of psychological strain.

Approach

How I work

Respectful, empathetic and appreciative interaction is, for me, the foundation of a trusting working relationship.

I believe that every person has their own strengths and resources that can help even in difficult phases of life.

My aim is to help people strengthen their personal resources, develop new perspectives and make their everyday life more independent and stable.

Individual needs, circumstances and goals are always at the centre.

Services

What I offer

  • SociotherapySupport for people with mental illness in coping with everyday life and strengthening their independence.
  • Psychological counsellingSupport with personal strain, life crises and challenging situations.
  • Intercultural couples therapySupport for Turkish-speaking couples with relationship conflicts, communication problems and culturally shaped challenges.
